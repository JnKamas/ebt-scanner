# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, discussions, reviews and pull requests. Explain technical disagreements and focus criticism on the work. Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Never post passwords, session cookies or personal banknote/location data.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation to protect the community. Do not retaliate against someone who raises a concern.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for abusive public content. If a concern requires private discussion, request a private contact channel from the maintainer without posting personal details publicly. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
