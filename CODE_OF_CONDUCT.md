# Code of Conduct

Be kind, be constructive and welcome people with different levels of experience.
Disagree with ideas without attacking the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption and sharing someone
else's private information are not welcome. Never post passwords, session
cookies or personal banknote/location data.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation. Do not retaliate
against anyone raising a concern. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse, or
ask the maintainer for a private contact channel without posting personal details.
For vulnerabilities, follow [Security](SECURITY.md).
